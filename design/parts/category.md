# Category

`sb-category`, `sb-category--1` to `--6` · `SbCategory`

The tone a person gave to a thing he files himself: a label, a board column, a project.

## States

- With a tone: a small dot in the tone before the word, or the tone on an icon inside. The word stays `text`.
- Without a tone: only the word.

## Rules

- Six fixed muted tones. The tone always stands beside its word; never a fill, never a text colour, never the only
  cue. [FAR-12]
- Only for what the person files himself, never for a state the app assigns. [FAR-12]
