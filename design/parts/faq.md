# Frequent questions

`sb-faq` · web only

A list of questions on a website; each opens its answer in place. Evidence for a claim is a claim row, not this
part.

## States

- Closed: the question in strong weight with a chevron at the end.
- Open: the chevron turned, the answer under the question.

## Behaviour

- Each question is a row of at least 56 high with hairlines between the rows; the list keeps the reading measure.
- It needs no script: Enter and Space open and close, and the open state is the browser's own.
- The browser's search can open a closed answer.
- The chevron turns only when the reader opens or closes a row, never on load, and not at all with reduced motion.
- Printing opens every answer.

## Rules

- An answer has at most three sentences, says „du“ and ends in a link. [WEB-07]
- Hairlines stand between and around the rows; no row gets a fill or a card.
