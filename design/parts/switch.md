# Switch

`sb-switch`, `sb-switch-row` · `SbSwitchRow`

A setting that is on or off and takes effect at once.

## States

- Off: strong edge, thumb at the start. On: accent edge, thumb at the end with a check, so „on“ never rests on
  colour alone.
- Hover thickens the track's edge, focus shows the ring on the track, disabled takes the weak edge.

## Behaviour

- The whole row toggles and is at least 48 high; the label names the switch.
- A help sentence may stand under the row, an icon before the label.
- When label and switch no longer fit on one line (large text, narrow phone), the switch moves under the label and
  stays at the end. No word is broken.

## Rules

- Use it for a setting that applies immediately. A choice that waits for „Speichern“ is a checkbox.
