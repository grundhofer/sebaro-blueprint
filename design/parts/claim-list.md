# Claim list

`sb-claim-list` · web only

Several claims of a website one below the other, each with its note digit. Use it where a page lists facts; a
claim inside a sentence just takes its digit. Shared rules: Evidence.

## Behaviour

- One claim per row in strong weight, with its digit at the end.
- Hairlines divide the rows.

## Rules

- A row whose note may not be stated is left out; a disclosure always stays. [K27]
- Each claim states exactly the value of its fact. [BEL-06]
