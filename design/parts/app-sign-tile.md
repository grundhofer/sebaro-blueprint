# App sign tile, app grid, app list

`sb-sign-tile`, `sb-app-grid`, `sb-app-card`, `sb-app-list`, `sb-app-row` · `SbSignTile` (grid and list: web only)

The tile identifies an app. Grid and list show several apps on a website.

## Behaviour

- The tile comes ready-made from the product's kit in three sizes and draws its colours itself. Below the smallest
  size the small form of the sign is used.
- When the app's name stands next to the tile, the tile is hidden from screen readers; otherwise the tile itself
  speaks the name.
- App grid: columns divided by hairlines, one on a phone, two on a tablet, four on a desktop, without a card fill
  or frame. Each app shows tile, name, one-liner and its tags.
- App list: rows with the tile at the start, then name, one-liner and a meta line such as „Android · Verfügbar ·
  Fakten“; a hairline above each row, two columns on a desktop.

## Rules

- The tile always stands beside the app's visible name or, in a top bar, the view's title. [K25]
- Never recolour, rotate, stretch or outline the sign; the tile carries only the symbol, no words. [K25]
- The product colour lives in the tile; it is not used for the grid or the list around it. [K5]
