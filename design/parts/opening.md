# Opening

`sb-opening`, `sb-product-area--picture` · web only

The top of a website's start page: a large heading on the neutral ground, then one sentence and the one primary
action. The product's picture follows after it, outside the opening.

## Behaviour

- The heading grows with the width of the page, from a phone size to a large desktop size, in the same heavy weight
  in light and dark.
- Whole words always fit their column: a title that would be too wide is set smaller until they do. Without scripts
  an editorial title has to fit by its wording.
- Under the heading stands the zone: a short sentence first, the primary action last, an ink line above. On phones
  and at large text it wraps, and the action stays at the end.
- The picture stands in a product area that holds exactly one picture: an image with a useful alt text, or a
  specimen that cannot be operated, on a neutral ground of its own inside a frame.

## Rules

- No fixed height, no clipping, no ellipsis, no sideways scrolling and no coloured band in the opening.
- The heading takes the level the document needs; the look comes from the opening, not from the level.
- No second framed action in the zone.
- Nothing stands on the product ground itself: no copy, no caption. No link, button or other control stands in or
  around the picture, not even inside a specimen. Captions and actions stand outside. [K5]
- It is the one product area of its view. [K5]
- Tight line spacing is no reason to cut accents or umlauts.
- Claims in the opening carry their evidence references as everywhere. [K27]
