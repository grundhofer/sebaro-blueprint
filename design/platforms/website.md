# Website

What holds for a website beyond the core rules and the view types.

## Head and foot

- The header holds, in this order, the tile with the wordmark, the site's own few words, „DE / EN“ and the button
  „Hell oder Dunkel“ ([theme-button](../parts/theme-button.md)); on a phone in two rows, without a menu button.
  [NAV-06, NAV-07, WEB-02]
- The first focus stop is the skip link „Zum Inhalt“. [ZUG-06]
- The footer always links „Impressum“, „Datenschutz“ and „Lizenzen“, „GitHub“ only with a public repository, and
  offers „Farbton: Kühl · Warm“ under „Darstellung“. [WEB-03]

## Pages and language

- No start in the product colour: a website is readable at once. [K29]
- The start page is `overview`, a list of pages `list`, a text page `read`, legal pages and „Über“ `about`.
- „DE / EN“ leads to the same page in the other language; a page that does not exist shows its code grey and
  without a link. No redirect by the browser's language, no flags. [WEB-10]

## Layout and type

- Two page breakpoints, 640 and 1024 px. Parts adapt to their own place, not to the page. [RAU-03]
- A heading takes the look of its role, not of its level. [SCH-07]
- Icons keep their size at every text size; only text grows. [ICO-04]

## Print and sharing

- Print is always light and shows only the content; external links print their address. [WEB-04, WEB-11]
- Every page with its own title has its own share image, built from its title and description. [WEB-09]
